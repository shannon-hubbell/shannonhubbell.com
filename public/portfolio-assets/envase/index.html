Envase Lite: The Next-Gen App for Digital Drayage

Next-Generation Digital Drayage

Envase Lite is a cloud-based, comprehensive drayage Transportation Management System (TMS) tailor-made for small intermodal carriers and owner operators. Envase Lite empowers drivers and dispatchers to optimize every step of their workflow in one easy-to-use platform. If you move containers, Envase Lite is your co-pilot.

FAQ's

Who can use Envase90?

Envase Lite is optimized for motor carriers with one to five drivers.

How much does it cost?

Operations and Admin: The first user is free. Each additional user costs $49 per month for up to 3 additional users.

Drivers: $15 a month per driver to add the mobile app.

How can I install the app?

You can download the Envase90 app on Google Play and Apple App Store.

What if I have five or more drivers?

For fleets of five or more trucks, contact info@envasetechnologies.com to discuss your needs.

Can I connect Envase90 with other TMS platforms?

Yes. Envase Lite integrates directly with other terminals, partners, tracing services, and TMSes — including those within the Envase family: Compcare Services, GTG, Profit Tools, and Tailwind.

Where is Envase Lite available?

Envase Lite is available anywhere loads can be offered, assigned, and accepted across North America.

Who Makes Envase Lite?

Envase Lite is a product of Envase Technologies, whose mission is to deliver a single, streamlined system that digitizes the lifecycle of a container across the intermodal ecosystem. For more info, visit www.envasetechnologies.com.

How do I get support?

For help to download, install, or use Envase Lite, please contact support@envaselite.com.
